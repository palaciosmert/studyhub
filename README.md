# studyhub
Course, assignment and exam tracking app for students


## Problem
- University students take several courses each semester, and every course
comes with its own assignments, projects and exam dates. These deadlines
are usually announced in different places, such as lectures, course
websites, emails and messaging groups, so students end up keeping track
of them in scattered notes or simply trying to remember them. As a result,
important deadlines are easily forgotten, exams are noticed at the last
minute, and students feel stressed and disorganized.

## Target Users
- Studyhub is designed for university students mainly. But also it can be very useful for high school students.

## Solution
- By using Studyhub students can add their courses. Inside courses they can add their assignments to complete or upcoming exams separately. With Studyhub students can track their progress easily.
